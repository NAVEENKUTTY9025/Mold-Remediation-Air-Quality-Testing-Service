Our Story

From a small basement lab to Salem's most trusted name in air quality.

15+ Years of experience
2,400+ Homes remediated
100% Clearance test pass rate

"I started PureAir after watching my own family get sick from hidden mold. I knew there had to be a better, more scientific way — one that homeowners could actually trust."

— Dr. Elena Marsh, Founder

PureAir was founded in 2011 with a simple belief: mold remediation shouldn't rely on guesswork. We built our company on lab-grade testing, third-party verification, and honest communication. What began as a one-person operation in a basement lab is now a full-service team of 12+ certified technicians, scientists, and remediation experts.

Today, we serve homeowners, property managers, and business owners across Salem and surrounding areas. Every project still follows the same principles we started with: measure, remediate, verify, and protect. No shortcuts, no hidden fees, no false promises.

Our mission & core values

Three principles guide everything we do — from the first phone call to the final clearance report.

01

Science Over Guesswork

Every diagnosis is backed by lab data, thermal imaging, and moisture readings. We never remediate without proof.

02

Honesty Without Upsell

If you don't need remediation, we'll tell you. We'd rather earn a lifetime client than a one-time invoice.

03

Health First, Always

We treat every home like our own. Safe containment, EPA-approved products, and rigorous clearance testing are non-negotiable.
