FAQ

Common questions

How long does this service take?

Standard inspections take 1-2 hours. Full remediation projects typically take 2-5 days depending on the extent of the mold. We'll give you an accurate timeline after the initial assessment.

Do I need to leave my home during the work?

For inspections, no. For remediation work involving significant mold, we recommend vacating the affected area (or home) for safety. We'll advise you specifically based on your situation.

Will my insurance cover this?

Many homeowner policies cover mold remediation when it results from a covered peril (like a burst pipe). We provide the documentation your insurer needs, and can bill them directly in most cases.

What if mold comes back after treatment?

If mold returns within 5 years due to defects in our work, we'll re-treat the affected area at no cost. However, mold caused by new water damage or neglected maintenance isn't covered — but we'll always help you fix it.

Can I get a written estimate before booking?

Absolutely. Every estimate is free and provided in writing. We'll never pressure you into booking on the spot.
