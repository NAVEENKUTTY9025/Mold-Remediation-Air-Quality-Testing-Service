A proven 4-step process from inspection to clean air

Every project follows a strict, certified protocol — from the first inspection through final clearance testing — so you know exactly what's happening at every stage.

1

Inspection & Moisture Mapping

Day 1

Thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters locate hidden mold and water intrusion. Air and surface samples are collected for laboratory analysis.

2

Containment & Removal

Day 2–3

Negative air pressure and HEPA filtration prevent cross-contamination. Contaminated materials are safely removed and disposed of per EPA guidelines.

3

Cleaning & Antimicrobial Treatment

Day 3–4

All surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, wiped, and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions to eliminate remaining spores.

4

Clearance Testing & Report

Day 5

Independent post-remediation verification confirms the mold is gone. You receive a detailed report plus a prevention plan.
