Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you inspect my property?

Same-day inspections are usually available when booked before 3pm. Emergency response within 4 hours for water damage situations.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We provide detailed documentation, photos, and lab reports that insurers accept. We can also bill your insurer directly.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes — the initial visual inspection and written estimate are always free. Lab sample analysis (if needed) has a small fee.

How long does remediation take?

Most residential projects take 2–5 days from containment to clearance. Larger commercial jobs may take 1–2 weeks.

Will you test the air after remediation?

Absolutely. Post-remediation verification testing is included in every remediation project — you get a clearance report.

Need urgent help?

Our emergency line is open 24/7 for active water damage and visible mold outbreaks.
